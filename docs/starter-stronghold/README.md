## Starter stronghold art integration

After #YPMIZ was verified in production and completed, the enclosure now uses the corrected imagegen level-1 timber palisade. Twelve existing wall runs keep their tile positions and gateway opening. End-post centers are registered at source (46,361)/(342,215) on a padded 388 × 370 canvas. A mirrored sprite supplies the opposite direction. Each run spans five tiles; source padding, vertical scale and shear place both ground contacts exactly on the 2:1 ground line. Collision uses continuous authored columns rather than alpha gaps between logs. Decorative stone bastions and the loose broken wall are omitted. The Pixi editor's Stronghold components use the same sprites and transforms, without rewriting published map or draft records.

Stronghold Core retains #YPMIZ's approved center, stable collision and saved-level artwork. The gate and its animation, combat, collision, health and progression are unchanged. Enclosure art is the default terrain presentation; it does not grant a constructed wall record or bypass the published Core-3 wall construction rule. No automatic wall art upgrade is inferred from Core level.

Stormshade Watchtower and Runeseekers Runetower use simple level-1 imagegen timber lookouts. Runetower has a small orb cradle. The shared 640 × 900 canvas places the ground baseline at y=860. Existing faction, Core-12, resource, duplicate and solo-play construction checks, attack cadence, targeting, damage and save fields are unchanged. No new tower is granted; only a legally constructed or already saved permitted defense renders. No Core relocation or invented Main Hall unlock is included.

## Raster provenance and prompt

Built-in imagegen used the actual gameplay Barracks and Forge plus the corrected palisade as primary material/camera references; old Watchtower and Runetower were identity references only. Prompt: two level-1 defense sprites on a transparent sheet, separate left Watchtower and right Runetower, equal footprint and common ground baseline; weathered matte timber, restrained dark iron, angular painted medieval materials, 2:1 isometric camera, upper-left daylight. Raised four-post braced timber platforms, front ladder, plain railing and small stone footings. Watchtower open top; Runetower the same simple structure plus a small blue orb in a plain iron cradle. Remove snow, glossy orange varnish, large magical VFX, flying bolts, ornate elite decoration, text and background. No 3D asset pipeline. The generated source is retained as tower-source.png; extraction, transparent canvas registration and mirroring are deterministic packaging only.

## Release safety

Published terrain previously rendered above the baked court and hid its walls and Core. Terrain now renders below the court. The baked surface preserves transparency outside the fixed court; lighting retains that alpha coverage after tinting, so published terrain remains visible outside the stronghold. This fixes draw order without changing published map data. Browser validation compares a 32 × 32 rendered court region to the CPU bake and checks transparent outside pixels, as well as construction gates and persistence.

An isolated task worktree builds the latest cumulative game and editor. Shipping acquires the existing arkodia-deploy.lock, requires the live build to be an ancestor of the release and publishes through the existing release publisher. Existing layers, durable GAME_SAVE_DIR, published map revision and draft records are retained. No stale map snapshot or gameplay save ships in the release.
