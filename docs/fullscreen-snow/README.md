## Full-screen Stronghold

The Stronghold world now occupies the entire viewport as one Canvas2D scene. Terrain, walls, vegetation and all seven buildings are drawn into that canvas. HTML is reserved for overlays: resources, building dock, camera control, destination buttons, an optional building panel and a menu. No sidebar or forge-variant strip reserves world space. The artwork from the modular quality revision is reused.

Drag to pan; scroll or pinch to zoom; Fit world shows the whole fort. Select a building directly from visible sprite pixels, including roofs, or use the keyboard-accessible building dock. Selection opens the floating panel and moves the camera to the building. Close the panel to uncover the world. On portrait screens, the panel becomes a scrollable bottom sheet, and the body never scrolls. The asset editor, older travel map, backups and forge appearance gallery remain in the overlay menu.

## Expedition I — Frostpass

The Expedition I destination and Expedition Table enter `/snow-expedition.html`. This is a playable authored snow level, separate from the older procedural travel-map prototype. It uses PixiJS and the existing animated hero/revenant sets with the snowy modular trees, rocks, paving details, camp cart and fire.

1. Begin at the blue camp marker.
2. Recover the three golden supply-cache markers: Lost outpost, Frozen ridge and Eastern trail.
3. Defeat all six revenants. This finite patrol does not respawn.
4. Return to the campfire and interact to extract.

WASD/arrows move, Space attacks and E interacts. The screen also provides a movement pad on mobile, Attack and Interact buttons, optional auto combat, minimap and objective counters. Tapping the ground moves toward that location; it is a direct movement target, not a pathfinding system. The camera follows the player. Snowfall respects reduced-motion preferences.

The first clear grants **150 Gold, 30 Mana and 5 Souls**. Existing Stronghold equipment/training/hero bonuses add health at entry. Reward history is stored separately from arena-run history, so pruning old arena entries cannot make the first-clear reward repeat. Resource credit uses the existing Web Locks transaction. Earlier Stronghold saves remain valid.

Progress, defeated enemies, recovered caches, health and position are checkpointed in this browser under `arkodia-snow-expedition-v1`. Death opens a defeat/retry screen. Restart clears only this expedition checkpoint and suppresses the page-exit save that would otherwise restore it. Returning home keeps the checkpoint. Completion can be replayed, but grants no second first-clear reward.

This first level is a bounded ten-by-ten clearing with a follow camera and decorative forest/rock perimeter. The terrain kit remains reusable; the level is not a generated campaign or backend-authoritative multiplayer state.
