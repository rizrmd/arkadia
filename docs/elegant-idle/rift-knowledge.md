## Visual direction

A nimble hooded adventurer with a rust-red scarf, dark teal cloth, light leather equipment, and a cyan crystal forearm emitter. A readable lightweight silhouette supports fast movement.

## Ability direction

- **Pulse Shot:** mid-range energy projectile.
- **Phase Dash:** short teleport with afterimages.
- **Rift Trap:** ground rift that pulls enemies inward before exploding.
- **Overdrive:** temporary empowered attacks and faster dashes.

## Production scope

This is a design reference, not a calibrated sprite atlas or evidence of smooth animation. Validate idle, running, dash, and one attack in motion before producing the full skill set. Keep consistent body scale, ground pivots, and readable anticipation, impact, and recovery.

## Elegant idle sprite sheet — eight directions × eight frames

![Rift Runner elegant idle sprite sheet, eight directions and eight animation frames](/api/rooms/DRb4787514f3224844f3c207ab/attachments/DA0addbe3d3b0d4d9e3631bdc0)

The completed sheet is embedded directly in Knowledge, as requested. This is a character art study for review; production game character assets were not replaced. The retained elegant hand-painted poster (DA29b8b200cef04aff12014c59) is the source for the tall graceful anatomy, embroidered long coat, refined boots and right-arm cyan Crystal Forearm Emitter. The poster above remains retained; purged old concepts were not restored.

Rows run **N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW** from top to bottom; columns are chronological idle frames **1–8**. PNG size: **3072 × 3072**, with transparent **384 × 384** cells and a registered foot pivot at **(192, 360)**. Each heading contains eight separately generated poses with breathing, hair/cloth movement and weapon-light variation. No mirrored headings or duplicated static frames are used.

All eight headings and their frames were visually inspected. Export validation passed for 64 unique frames, transparent margins, no occupied cell-border pixels and aligned lowest boot contacts. The source images include genuine alpha; the raw image viewer can display hidden background RGB, so light-background composites were also checked. Small generated costume-detail variations remain subject to art review.

Built-in imagegen produced the raster art. The isolated branch **preview/elegant-idle** was committed and pushed: **1de0e6d**, followed by route adjustment **5cd8de5**. Per the latest instruction, delivery is the inline Knowledge sheet; no additional preview is required. Source attempts and correction images are embedded inline in the existing source page below.
