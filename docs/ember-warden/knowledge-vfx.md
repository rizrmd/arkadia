## Separate transparent effects

[Open aligned playback](https://arkadia-custom-whachat.fural.space/ember-warden-review/). These are deterministic code-authored VFX rendered to transparent sheets, not independent imagegen body art. Flame Fan follows the per-frame right-wrist socket; trail, seal and aura use the common ground pivot. VFX cells are 384px with origin (192,243.2), body cells 256px with pivot (128,179.2).

Body events trigger emission; stationary Flame Fan sustains frames 5–7 and stops before recovery. The moving attack has a separate continuous effect loop. The seal first appears at activation and erupts later. Front/back compositing uses facing and does not provide per-pixel limb occlusion. These are visual proposals, not gameplay balance.
