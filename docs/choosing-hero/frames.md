## Placement and sizing

Built with the built-in imagegen tool from the four original Choosing Hero attachments. Generated masters are preserved; runtime exports use sRGB PNG and straight alpha. The original Choosing Hero page and its four references remain unchanged.

Use the default card frame for unselected heroes and the glowing frame for the selected hero. Hollow centers allow portrait art to remain separate. Gold corner ornaments are included in frame edges; the compass crest and divider are also standalone transparent elements.

The mobile panel export uses a nine-slice assembly to retain smaller corners around a wider black content area. Use a 64 px inset on its 800 × 400 canvas when resizing. Keep the four corners fixed, stretch edge strips only along their long axis, and stretch the center. Prefer uniform scaling for hero frames and the crest; crop or repeat divider rails to preserve finial proportions.
